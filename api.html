How it works

From your first scan to full peace of mind

No agents to install. No server access needed. SiteTrace watches your sites from the outside — exactly like a real visitor would — and tells you when something breaks.

1. Add any website

Paste a URL into SiteTrace. That's the entire setup. SiteTrace runs an instant full-page audit — no plugins, no server credentials, no code changes.

Any website No install Results in seconds

2. SiteTrace runs a full health scan

Every scan checks over 20 signals across five categories: availability, performance, SEO fundamentals, security headers, and domain health.

Uptime & HTTP status SSL cert & expiry Title, meta, H1, canonical Open Graph & viewport HSTS, CSP, X-Frame Domain & DNS changes

3. You get a clear health score

Issues are ranked by severity. Downtime is critical. A missing H1 is a warning. You always know what to fix first. A 0–100 health score makes it easy to track progress and share with clients.

4. Monitoring runs automatically

With a paid plan, SiteTrace keeps checking your site on a schedule. If something breaks — downtime, SSL change, keyword disappears — you get an email alert within minutes. A second failing check must confirm the issue before an incident opens, preventing false alarms.

Email alerts Slack & Teams False-alarm protection

5. Share results with clients

Enable a public status page for any monitored site and share the link with your client. They see live uptime, response time, and incident history — no login required. Download a health report PDF as proof of your monthly maintenance work.

What SiteTrace looks for

Every check covers the signals most likely to hurt uptime, SEO performance, or client trust.

Uptime & response time

Detect outages and slowdowns before clients notice. Retry logic prevents false positives from temporary blips.

SSL certificate

Days until expiry, valid chain, HTTPS redirect enforced. Alerts at 30, 14, and 7 days before expiry.

SEO fundamentals

Title, meta description, canonical, H1, noindex flag, Open Graph, viewport — with platform-aware intelligence for YouTube, GitHub, and others.

Domain & DNS

Domain expiry countdown plus DNS record change detection — catch unauthorized changes to A, MX, and NS records.

Custom keyword monitoring

Set a phrase that must be present (or absent) on the page. Ideal for tracking product availability, pricing text, or critical content.

Security headers

Missing CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, and X-Content-Type-Options — common sources of vulnerability reports.

Incident management

Smart alerts that don't wake you up for nothing.

SiteTrace waits for a second confirming failure before it opens an incident — no false alarms from network blips.

  • First failure recorded — watching for confirmation, no alert yet
  • Second consecutive failure — incident opens, alert sent via email + Slack
  • Site recovers — incident resolved automatically, recovery alert sent
  • Maintenance windows — pause incident detection during planned downtime
